Microsoft 365 Copilot · Lab 1

Keep a Teams Meeting on Agenda with Copilot

Use standard Copilot or Facilitator to compare a real discussion with its planned agenda, identify drift and close with a human-confirmed meeting record.

Level 100 Duration 25–35 min Format Guided practice
Workshop path: Documents, communication and meetings

Lab details

LevelPersonaDurationPurpose
100Meeting chair, project manager, business analyst25–35 minutesKeep a real Teams discussion aligned to an agreed agenda and finish with verified decisions and actions.

Introduction

An agenda is useful only when the meeting keeps returning to it. Copilot can compare what is being discussed with the planned topics, reveal missing decisions and help create a parking lot. It does not replace the chair: people must still confirm when an item is closed, who owns an action and what deadline was agreed.

This lab gives you two routes. Choose the one available in your Microsoft 365 tenant.

OptionBest whenWhat participants see
A — Standard CopilotCopilot is available inside the Teams meeting.Your questions and Copilot responses are private to you unless you share them.
B — FacilitatorThe Facilitator agent is enabled for the tenant and meeting.The agenda, timer and AI-generated notes can support the whole meeting.
Note

Facilitator is a Teams meeting agent. It does not appear as a separate option inside the Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat app.

Learning objectives

After completing this lab, you will be able to:

  • turn a general agenda into a decision-ready agenda;
  • select the correct Copilot meeting mode for the required retention;
  • track covered, current and missing agenda items;
  • separate facts, proposals, decisions and unresolved questions;
  • move unrelated discussion into a parking lot;
  • verify actions, owners and deadlines before closing the meeting.

Prerequisites

  • A Microsoft 365 account with an eligible Microsoft Copilot licence.
  • Microsoft Teams desktop or web access.
  • A scheduled internal Teams meeting that you can edit.
  • Permission to use Copilot and, when needed, transcription.
  • For Option B: Facilitator allowed by the Teams administrator and Loop experiences enabled.
Before using real data

Follow your organisation’s policies for recording, transcription, retention and sensitive information. Tell participants when transcription or AI assistance is used.

Running scenario

You chair a 30-minute Project Atlas readiness meeting. The team must decide whether the new internal service desk portal can enter user acceptance testing and agree the remaining actions. During the discussion, people may introduce future functionality, disagree about dates or sound as if they agree without confirming an owner or deadline.

Decision-ready agenda

TimeTopicRequired outcome
3 minOpening and readiness criteriaConfirm the meeting objective and decisions required today.
8 minSSO and security readinessDecide whether UAT may start with the open timeout test.
8 minUAT environment and start dateConfirm the environment delivery date and UAT start date.
6 minData migration validationAgree an owner and deadline.
5 minClose and parking lotRead back decisions, actions, unresolved items and topics for follow-up.
Design principle

Each agenda item should state the required outcome. “Discuss security” is vague; “decide whether UAT may start with the open timeout test” gives Copilot and the chair a clear completion test.

Exercise 1: Prepare the meeting

1.1 Add an authoritative agenda

  1. Open Teams Calendar and create or open a scheduled meeting.
  2. Add the meeting objective and decision-ready agenda to the invitation.
  3. If available, select Add an agenda to place it in the collaborative meeting notes.
  4. Also post the final agenda in the meeting chat if you plan to use standard Copilot. This gives you a precise source to name in the prompt.
  5. Do not place sensitive production data in a practice meeting.

1.2 Choose the retention mode

  1. Open the meeting and select Options.
  2. Open Copilot and other AI.
  3. Choose one setting:
SettingUse it whenImportant effect
During and after the meetingYou need to ask questions after the meeting or retain the Copilot history.Transcription is required. Copilot starts when transcription starts.
Only during the meetingYou need live help but do not need the untranscribed spoken content afterwards.Copilot can use speech-to-text during the meeting. Untranscribed content and live Copilot interactions are unavailable after it ends.
OffAI assistance is not appropriate for this meeting.Copilot is disabled for the meeting.
Human decision

Do not select a mode only for convenience. Choose it according to the sensitivity of the discussion, the need for a post-meeting record and your organisation’s policy.

Exercise 2A: Track the agenda with standard Copilot

Use this route when the Copilot button is available in the Teams meeting controls. Your Copilot pane is a private working space.

2A.1 Start the control loop

  1. Join the scheduled Teams meeting.
  2. Confirm the objective and agenda aloud.
  3. Start transcription if you selected During and after the meeting.
  4. Select Copilot in the meeting controls.
  5. Paste the tracking prompt below.
Initial agenda-tracking prompt
Use the agenda posted in the meeting chat as the authoritative agenda for this meeting.

Help me compare the actual discussion with that agenda. Track:
1. Current agenda item
2. Items already discussed
3. Items not yet discussed
4. Confirmed decisions
5. Proposed decisions that have not been confirmed
6. Action items, owners and deadlines
7. Off-topic subjects for a parking lot

Use only evidence from the meeting conversation and chat.
Do not mark an item complete unless someone explicitly confirms it is closed.
Do not invent an owner, deadline or decision. Write “Not confirmed” when information is missing.

2A.2 Run short checkpoints

Do not ask Copilot continuously. Use deliberate checkpoints when moving between agenda items or when the conversation starts to drift.

Checkpoint: What is happening now?

Current position
Compare the discussion so far with the agenda.

Tell me:
- Which agenda item are we discussing now?
- What facts have been established?
- What decision is still required?
- What one question should I ask before closing this item?

Separate facts from proposals.

Checkpoint: Is the decision complete?

Decision quality
Has a decision been explicitly confirmed for the current agenda item?

Show:
- the proposed or confirmed decision;
- the evidence and speaker;
- any missing owner, deadline or condition;
- whether the item is ready for the chair to close.

Do not treat agreement-like language such as “sounds good” as final confirmation unless the decision itself is clear.

Checkpoint: Park the drift

Parking lot
Identify recent discussion that does not belong to the current agenda item.

Create:
1. A short parking-lot entry
2. A neutral sentence I can use to redirect the meeting
3. The agenda question we should return to

Checkpoint: Preserve disagreement

Conflicting information
What information currently conflicts?

Do not resolve the conflict yourself. Show:
- Statement A and its speaker
- Statement B and its speaker
- The impact of the conflict
- The clarification question the chair should ask

Exercise 2B: Track the agenda with Facilitator

Use this route when Facilitator is enabled for your organisation and available in the scheduled Teams meeting.

2B.1 Turn on Facilitator

  1. Open the scheduled meeting in Teams Calendar.
  2. Select Options → Copilot and other AI.
  3. Turn on Facilitator and apply the setting. Depending on your Teams version and permissions, you may also be able to turn it on during the meeting from More actions.
  4. Keep the structured agenda in the invitation or meeting notes. Facilitator can also receive it in the meeting chat.
  5. Join with another participant. Confirm that the agenda timer or AI-generated notes become visible.
Provide the agenda in chat
@Facilitator, here is the agreed agenda:

1. Opening and readiness criteria — 3 minutes
2. SSO and security readiness — 8 minutes
3. UAT environment and start date — 8 minutes
4. Data migration validation — 6 minutes
5. Actions, unresolved items and parking lot — 5 minutes

Please track this agenda during the meeting.

2B.2 Use shared agenda and timer commands

Add or change an item
@Facilitator, add “Training and communications” to the parking lot.
Start a closing timer
@Facilitator, add a timer for five minutes for the final action review.
Update an agenda allocation
@Facilitator, change “Data migration validation” to four minutes.
Do not over-interpret the checkmark

Facilitator marks a topic after discussion has started. A checkmark does not prove that a decision was made, all questions were answered or the item is complete.

Exercise 3: Close with a verified record

Use this closing step with either option. Ask before the final two minutes, then read the result aloud and let participants correct it.

Agenda completion check
Run an agenda completion check.

Create four sections:
1. Confirmed decisions
2. Actions with confirmed owners and deadlines
3. Unresolved questions
4. Agenda items not covered

Use “Not confirmed” wherever information is missing.
Do not convert a suggestion into a decision.
  1. Read each decision aloud. Ask: “Is this the decision we made?”
  2. For every action ask the named owner to confirm the task and deadline.
  3. Preserve conflicts and unanswered questions rather than forcing a conclusion.
  4. Confirm the parking lot and who will decide its next step.
  5. State any agenda item that was not covered.
  6. Post the confirmed corrections in meeting chat or notes.
Final corrected meeting record
Update the final meeting record using only the corrections just confirmed by participants.

For every decision and action, include:
- Description
- Owner
- Deadline
- Evidence or speaker
- Status

Keep unresolved items and parking-lot topics in separate sections.

Success criteria

Your completed meeting record should contain:

Limitations and good practice

LimitationPractical response
Copilot cannot use an agenda stored only in someone’s private notes.Put the agreed agenda in the invitation, collaborative notes or meeting chat.
Discussion does not prove completion.Ask the chair and participants to explicitly confirm closure.
Copilot may infer an owner or deadline from context.Require “Not confirmed” for missing fields and ask the person to confirm aloud.
Speakers may disagree or use ambiguous language.Ask Copilot to preserve both statements and propose a clarification question.
Without a transcript, spoken content is not available for post-meeting questions.Use transcription when policy allows and a post-meeting record is required.
Facilitator availability varies by licence, policy and meeting type.Prepare Option A as the fallback.
Poor audio, overlapping speech or incorrect spoken language reduces accuracy.Confirm the language, speak clearly and review names, owners and dates.
External or cross-organisation meetings may restrict Copilot.Test availability before the workshop or important meeting.

Troubleshooting

I cannot see Facilitator

  1. Confirm you are looking in Microsoft Teams, not the Copilot Chat app.
  2. Use a scheduled meeting and check Options → Copilot and other AI.
  3. Confirm an eligible Microsoft Copilot licence is assigned.
  4. Ask the Teams administrator to allow the Facilitator app and enable Loop experiences.
  5. Use Option A if Facilitator is unavailable.

Copilot is available during the meeting but not afterwards

The meeting was probably set to Only during the meeting without transcription. For later questions about spoken content, a transcript must be available.

Copilot uses chat but not spoken discussion

Check whether transcription was started and whether the transcript is available. After a meeting, Copilot can use chat alone when no transcript exists, but it cannot reconstruct what was said aloud.

The output marks an item complete too early

Correct it explicitly: “Do not mark this item complete. The team discussed it, but the decision and owner are not confirmed.”

Test your understanding

  1. Why is “required outcome” more useful than a topic-only agenda?
  2. What is the difference between During and after and Only during?
  3. Why should Copilot separate a proposal from a confirmed decision?
  4. What does a Facilitator agenda checkmark actually mean?
  5. Which fields must a person confirm before an action is accepted?
Lab complete

You have used a repeatable control loop: prepare the agenda, observe the discussion, compare it with the plan, redirect when needed and verify the final record with people.
