Source channel does not equal authority

A Teams message, transcript statement or Copilot recap may be useful evidence, but its location does not make it approved. Trust depends on the original source, speaker, decision rights, wording, date, conflicts and explicit confirmation.

Case: Project Nova

A company wants to replace email, Teams messages and local spreadsheets with one controlled portal for employee and contractor access requests. The discovery evidence is mixed: approved policy, a decision log, current-process documentation, interviews, a Teams workshop transcript, in-meeting chat, later post-meeting corrections and an AI-generated recap.

The recap sounds confident but contains five incorrect or unsupported conclusions. Your agent must find the original evidence, keep contradictions visible and produce traceable candidate requirements without pretending that every statement is approved.

ChallengeCorrect agent behaviourHuman responsibility
Different sources say 15 and 30 minutes.Identify privileged and standard sessions separately; cite policy and decision evidence.Confirm the approved distinction.
Legacy process says three-year retention; current policy says seven.Rank current policy above the superseded instruction and propose one field change.Approve the field-level delta.
Meeting discussion proposes a one-business-day SLA.Keep it Proposed or Not confirmed.Sponsor decides.
The recap says AI recommendations are in MVP.Reject the recap claim and cite original scope evidence.Product owner protects scope.

Learning objectives

  • create a declarative Requirements Discovery agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot;
  • ground it in specific Project Nova files, a meeting and selected Teams chats when available;
  • apply the TRUST framework to mixed-quality evidence;
  • distinguish an original statement, correction, decision, interpretation and AI summary;
  • conduct one-question-at-a-time discovery rather than jumping to solutions;
  • produce traceable candidate business, functional, non-functional, data and integration requirements;
  • preserve conflicts and name the authorised decision owner;
  • apply only approved changes directly in Excel and verify unchanged fields;
  • red-team the agent before controlled sharing.

The TRUST framework

Use TRUST for every material statement before it becomes a candidate requirement or register update.

GateQuestionRequired record
T - TraceWhere did this information come from?Source title or link, date, speaker or author, and exact supporting evidence.
R - RankHow authoritative and current is the source?Source type, current version, owner and the speaker's decision rights.
U - UncertaintyWhat is its evidence status?Confirmed, Proposed, Assumption, Conflict, Not confirmed, Superseded or Out of scope.
S - Sign-offWho can authorise the interpretation or change?Named decision owner and the confirmation still required.
T - TransferIs it safe to enter the controlled register?All gates passed, exact field-level delta and human approval.
Copilot recap rule

A recap is a navigation aid, not the original source. Every material recap claim must be traced back to the transcript, chat, policy, decision log or other original evidence before use.

Project Nova source hierarchy

PrioritySourceTreatment
1Current approved Security and Audit PolicyAuthoritative constraint within scope.
2Approved Decision LogAuthoritative decisions and named owners.
3Approved Business BriefApproved scope and objectives.
4Current Process and application catalogueObserved baseline and technical evidence; not automatic approval.
5Teams transcript and meeting or post-meeting chatEvidence requiring speaker, wording, date and authority checks.
6Stakeholder interview notesCandidate needs and questions; not approved by location alone.
7Copilot recap or answerNavigation and synthesis only; verify against original evidence.
Later can be stronger, but not automatically

A later post-meeting Teams message can legitimately correct an earlier transcript statement when the author has authority and explicitly states the correction. Record both items, the correction relationship and the decision owner. Do not silently replace the history.

Exercise 1: Prepare controlled knowledge

  1. Create a SharePoint folder named Project Nova - Requirements Discovery.
  2. Add all participant case documents and the application catalogue. Do not add the trainer answer key.
  3. Keep filenames, owners, dates and versions visible. These are part of the evidence.
  4. Open the Security and Audit Policy and Approved Decision Log first. They define the strongest controls.
  5. Review the transcript, in-meeting chat and post-meeting discussion as separate evidence items.
Two Teams sources

The spoken meeting and its associated chat can contain different information. A post-meeting correction may be stronger than an earlier verbal statement, but only after authority and explicit wording are checked.

Exercise 2: Create the agent

  1. Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot app.
  2. In the left navigation, under Agents, select New Agent.
  3. Select Skip to configure when that route is available.
  4. Enter Requirements Discovery as the name.
  5. Paste the description and the full supplied instructions.
  6. Set web search to Off for this controlled workshop case.
  7. Turn on Only use specified sources when available. Treat this as a grounding preference, not a replacement for citation checks.
Agent description
Analyse Project Nova evidence, conduct structured requirements discovery and produce traceable candidate requirements, conflicts and open questions using the TRUST framework.

Source inventory
Inventory every configured Project Nova source. For each source, return:
- source title or link;
- source type;
- date or version;
- owner, author or speaker;
- current or superseded status;
- decision authority;
- material conflicts;
- information that is still missing.

Do not produce requirements yet.

Exercise 4: Challenge the Copilot meeting recap

Do this before revealing the trainer answer key. The recap sounds plausible, but every one of its material conclusions is wrong, incomplete or unsupported.

Recap verification
Review the Project Nova Copilot meeting recap against the original transcript, in-meeting chat, post-meeting discussion, approved policy and decision log.

For every material recap statement, return:
1. the exact recap claim;
2. the original evidence that supports or contradicts it;
3. source title, date and speaker or author;
4. authority and evidence status;
5. whether the claim is Confirmed, Contradicted, Unsupported, Incomplete or Not confirmed;
6. the human decision owner or next verification step.

Never use the recap itself as proof.
Recap claimExpected classificationWhy
30-minute timeout for every userContradictedPolicy and decision log require 15 minutes for privileged access.
AI approval recommendations are in MVPContradictedApproved decision keeps AI recommendations outside MVP.
Evidence retention is three yearsContradictedCurrent approved policy requires seven years.
All five applications support real-time integrationContradictedFinanceHub has no approved real-time API.
One-business-day SLA is approvedNot confirmedThe transcript and chat show a proposal and pending decision.

Exercise 5: Conduct one-question-at-a-time discovery

Discovery interview
Conduct a requirements discovery interview for the standard-access approval process.

Ask one focused question at a time. Explore actor, trigger, information, business rule, exception, outcome and measure. After each answer:
- restate the fact or proposal;
- cite the evidence when one exists;
- state its TRUST status;
- identify any ambiguity or conflict;
- ask the next highest-value question.

Do not design a solution or create final requirements until I ask you to consolidate.

Answer at least five questions using the supplied evidence. Notice whether the agent asks for measurable definitions when terms such as “fast,” “easy” or “secure” appear.

Exercise 6: Produce traceable candidate requirements

Candidate requirements
Consolidate the verified Project Nova evidence into candidate requirements.

Create separate sections for Business, Functional, Non-functional, Business-rule, Data and Integration requirements. For every candidate return:
- Candidate ID and type;
- one testable requirement statement;
- rationale;
- draft acceptance criteria;
- original source title, date and speaker or author;
- supporting evidence;
- authority;
- TRUST evidence status;
- conflict or dependency;
- decision owner;
- approval status;
- open question.

Use Proposed unless an authoritative source explicitly approves the requirement. Preserve every conflict. Do not invent owners, deadlines, SLAs, interfaces or measures.
Quality gate

“The portal shall be easy to use” is not testable. The agent must ask the group to define an observable completion time, error rate, success measure or other acceptance criterion.

Exercise 7: Review before approval

Requirements quality review
Review every candidate requirement for necessity, clarity, atomicity, testability, measurability, feasibility, traceability, duplication, scope, conflict and decision authority.

Return only the defects and the smallest recommended correction. Do not silently rewrite an approved fact or resolve a business conflict.

Use the Requirements Quality and Approval Checklist. A candidate can move to the controlled register only after the source, authority, conflict and approval checks pass.

If direct editing is unavailable

Do not manually copy the agent's rows into Excel. Record the product or tenant limitation, keep the workbook unchanged and continue with the evaluation. Copilot in Excel supports plan, chat and direct editing experiences, but availability can vary by platform, licence and rollout.

Limitations and troubleshooting

IssueControl or fallback
Specific Meetings or Chats is not visible.Use the supplied transcript and chat files in the SharePoint folder. Record the capability gap.
The wrong meeting or chat appears.Remove it and scope the agent to the exact Project Nova item. Microsoft supports up to five specifically selected chats.
A participant cannot access a source.Do not widen permissions silently. Use authorised workshop files or ask the information owner.
The agent treats a chat as an approved decision.Strengthen the source hierarchy and require speaker authority plus explicit confirmation.
The agent cites the recap rather than original evidence.Reject the answer and rerun the recap-verification prompt.
Only use specified sources still allows general knowledge.Require citations for material claims and turn off web search. Treat the setting as a priority control, not absolute proof.
Excel edit mode is unavailable.Keep the file unchanged and record the limitation. Do not substitute manual copying.
Answers differ between participants.Compare evidence, status, authority and safety controls rather than identical wording.
